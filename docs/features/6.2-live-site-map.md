# Feature 6.2 – Live Site Map with Yield Score & Confidence

**Full deep dive:** https://claude.ai/artifact/5crUR5hVSYNFmu1GrDujp7 (source of truth)
**Leads:** Awais (data), Hassan (API), Salman (app), Remon (scoring) · **Co-owners:** Asmer, Sabih, Sayeem
**Sprints:** 1–2 (22 Oct – 17 Nov 2026) · **Status:** 📝 deep dive done · `data/` code ready

## In one paragraph
A picker opens the app and sees, on a map of the forests around them, how good each zone
is for a chosen berry right now: a yield score 0–100, a confidence level, a ripeness stage
and a plain-words reason. It is the home screen and the first thing every other feature
plugs into.

## Acceptance criteria (A1–A8)
A1 map < 3 s on 4G, `GET /zones` p95 < 300 ms · A2 every zone × species has a score ·
A3 5 colour bands + faded low confidence + legend · A4 zone sheet with ≥ 2 reasons ·
A5 species switch < 1 s · A6 works offline after one online session · A7 daily 07:00 +
within 5 min of a trusted report · A8 iOS 16+, Android 10+.
